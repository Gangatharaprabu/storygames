## Example: The Last Ferry (the test story)

1. **The Last Ferry**, test episode, 3 min. Mira must cross to the island tonight, but the
   last ferry is chained. Tone: quiet, warm dusk.
2. **Mira**, young traveller going to her mother's island. Wants to cross tonight.
3. **Tomo**, old ferryman. Gruff but fair. Short sentences. May return later.
4. Scenes: **Harbour at dusk** (Tomo, ferry, tackle box, lantern, noticeboard) → **Crossing** (on the ferry at night; the end).
5. Puzzles:
   - **Ferry lock** (harbour, full screen): three number dials, answer 3-1-4. Clue: the lantern
     (if you helped Tomo) or the noticeboard (if you didn't). Solving starts the crossing.
   - **Tackle box** (harbour, in the scene): two dials, answer 7-2 (Tomo's age, he says so).
     Solving gives rope.
6. Choice: help Tomo or insist. Helping → tackle box, rope, Tomo points to the lantern, and
   he waves goodbye at the end. Insisting → the noticeboard riddle, and he watches in silence.
7. Items: rope.
8. Carry-over: whether Mira helped Tomo.
